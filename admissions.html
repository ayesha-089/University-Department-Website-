Admissions

Start your academic journey with us.

General application steps

  1. Review the eligibility criteria announced by the university.
  2. Complete the official online or paper application.
  3. Upload or submit the required documents.
  4. Follow the official instructions for any test or interview.
  5. Check the merit list and fee submission schedule.

Commonly required documents

  • Academic transcripts or certificates
  • National identity document or applicable identification
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Any additional documents requested in the official admission notice

Always use the university's official admission advertisement for dates, fees, eligibility, and document requirements.
